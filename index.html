HOW TO PLAY

決闘の作法

  • Four spirits. Four relics. Every card on the altar belongs to someone.
  • On your turn, each living spirit may attack once. Tap a spirit, then ATTACK, then choose a target.
  • ATK vs DEF. If your ATK is higher, the defender is destroyed and the difference hits their life points.
  • If your ATK is lower, your attacker is destroyed and you take the difference.
  • Equal values destroy both cards.
  • If the enemy has no spirits, attack their life points directly.
  • Relics are single-use: heal, empower, shield, or burn. Activate them any time on your turn.
  • Reduce the enemy to 0 LP to win. Let your own reach zero and the shrine claims you.
