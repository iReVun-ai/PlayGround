A 24-zone card duel. Break the set, flip the board, and drive your rival's Life Points to zero.

01

THE MATRIX

Each duelist controls 6 Monster Zones and 6 Spell / Trap Zones — 24 zones in total. Six columns, four rows.

02

SET SUMMON

Once per turn, either Summon a monster face-up in Attack Position, or Set it face-down in Defense Position. Setting grants +200 LP.

03

FLIP & FIGHT

Click one of your face-down monsters to Flip Summon it. Flip effects fire. Face-up Attack monsters may attack once each.

04

DAMAGE

Attack a monster: higher ATK wins, and the difference hits the loser's LP. Attack a Defense monster with higher ATK and destroy it. No monsters? Attack directly.

05

SPELLS & TRAPS

Cast Spells from your hand into an empty Spell Zone. Set Traps face-down — they trigger automatically when your rival attacks.

06

WIN CONDITION

Reduce your opponent from 4000 LP to 0, or make them draw from an empty deck. Last duelist standing wins.
