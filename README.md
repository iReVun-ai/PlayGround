## 神殿決闘 — Temple Duel

A single-screen, shrine-themed card duel inspired by classic ATK/DEF monster battles.

- **16 square cards** on the altar: **8 large spirit cards** and **8 small relic cards** (four of each per duelist).
- Each spirit has **ATK / DEF**. Attack a spirit with higher ATK than its DEF to destroy it and carry the difference into the enemy's life points. Attack a stronger wall and your own spirit is destroyed instead.
- **4 relics** per side: heal 900, grant +800 ATK for the turn, raise a 1000-point barrier, or burn 700 LP directly. Each is single-use.
- Reduce the **Shadow Priest's 4000 LP to zero** to win. If both sides stall, the altar takes 300 LP from each duelist every round.
- Every card is square. Motion includes deal flips, idle float, pointer tilt, targeting marks, attack lunges, slashes, recoil, destruction bursts, LP countdowns, relic seals, and a full victory/defeat rite.

**Controls:** tap/click a spirit, press **ATTACK**, then choose an enemy spirit (or the enemy panel when their line is empty). Tap a relic and press **ACTIVATE**. **END TURN** passes the altar to the AI. Sound starts on your first interaction; the speaker button mutes it. `?` opens the rules.

Open `index.html` directly, or serve the folder with any static server (for example `python3 -m http.server 3002`) and visit `http://localhost:3002`.
